# Module 1: The Arrival
## Unit 1: First Words

**Description:** Learn greetings, politeness phrases, and basic communication for first interactions.

**Base Language:** English
**Target Language:** Serbian

---

## 1. Overview

Welcome to your first unit! This unit focuses on the absolute essentials you need for your first day in Serbia or Montenegro: greetings, basic politeness, and simple communication phrases.

#### A Note from the Founder (Jacksenn)

> "Let's be honest: at the beginning, learning a new language can be incredibly frustrating. It's hard to remember vocabulary, and you often feel like you'll never understand how to speak it. You might feel insecure in a restaurant or hesitate to use what you've learned. But here is our promise: Do not put pressure on yourself. You must have the courage to simply try. When people see that you are making an effort to speak their language, you will always bring a smile to their face. We are here to give you that courage."

#### Learning Objectives

By the end of this unit, you will be able to:

1. Greet people appropriately at different times of day
2. Say thank you, please, and excuse me politely
3. Introduce yourself with your name
4. Ask if someone speaks English
5. Say you don't understand or don't speak Serbian
6. Use basic yes/no responses

## 3. Grammar (Gramatika)

### A. Verb "biti" (to be) - Essential Forms

The verb **"biti"** (to be) is the most important verb in Serbian. For Unit 1, we focus on three essential forms.

| Person | Serbian | English | Negative |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Ja | sam | I am | nisam |
| Ti | si | You are | nisi |
| On/Ona | je | He/She is | nije |

**Examples:**
- Ja sam Alex. (I am Alex.)
- Ti si iz Srbije? (Are you from Serbia?)
- Ona nije turistkinja. (She is not a tourist.)
