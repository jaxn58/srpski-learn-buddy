## 1. Overview

#### A Note from the Founder (Jacksenn)

> "Let's be honest: at the beginning, learning a new language can be incredibly frustrating. It's hard to remember vocabulary, and you often feel like you'll never understand how to speak it. You might feel insecure in a restaurant or hesitate to use what you've learned. But here is our promise: **Do not put pressure on yourself.** You must have the courage to simply try. When people see that you are making an effort to speak their language, you will always bring a smile to their face. We are here to give you that courage."

#### Learning Objectives

By the end of this unit, **we** will be able to:

1. **Greet people** in formal and informal situations using appropriate Serbian phrases.
2. **Introduce yourself** and ask for someone's name using the verbs "biti" (to be) and "zvati se" (to be called).
3. **Ask for directions** and understand basic location-related questions ("Where is...?").
4. **Use essential survival vocabulary** for border crossings, rest stops, hotels, and tourist information centers.
5. **Recognize the gender of nouns** (Masculine, Feminine, Neuter) and understand why this matters for Serbian grammar.
6. **Navigate simple conversations** during your arrival, including greetings, asking for help, and saying goodbye.
7. **Understand the difference** between Serbian (Ekavian) and Montenegrin (Ijekavian) pronunciation and when to use each.

## 3. Grammar (Gramatik)

### 3.1 The Verb "biti" (to be)

The verb **biti** (to be) is the most important verb in Serbian. **We** use it constantly for introductions, descriptions, and locations.

#### Present Tense Conjugation

| **Person** | **Serbian** | **English** | **Example** |
|-----------|------------|-------------|-------------|
| ja | (ja) sam | I am | Ja sam turista. (I am a tourist.) |
| ti | (ti) si | you are (informal) | Ti si ovde*. / Ti si ovdje*. (You are here.) |
| on/ona/ono | on/ona/ono je | he/she/it is | On je na granici. (He is at the border.) |
| mi | (mi) smo | we are | Mi smo u hotelu. (We are in the hotel.) |
| vi | (vi) ste | you are (formal/plural) | Vi ste turista? (Are you a tourist?) |
| oni/one/ona | oni/one/ona su | they are | Oni su ovde*. / Oni su ovdje*. (They are here.) |

**\* Montenegrin (Ijekavian) variant:** "ovde" → "ovdje"

**Important:** In Serbian, **we** often drop the personal pronoun (ja, ti, mi) because the verb ending already tells us who is doing the action.

**Examples:**
- Sam turista. (I am a tourist.) — "Ja" is optional
- Ste ovde*? / Ste ovdje*? (Are you here?) — "Vi" is optional

### 3.2 The Verb "zvati se" (to be called)

The verb **zvati se** means "to be called" and is used for introducing yourself.

#### Present Tense Conjugation

| **Person** | **Serbian** | **English** | **Example** |
|-----------|------------|-------------|-------------|
| ja | zovem se | I am called / my name is | Zovem se Ana. (My name is Ana.) |
| ti | zoveš se | you are called | Kako se zoveš? (What is your name?) |
| on/ona/ono | zove se | he/she/it is called | On se zove Marko. (His name is Marko.) |
| mi | zovemo se | we are called | Zovemo se... (Our names are...) |
| vi | zovete se | you are called (formal/plural) | Kako se zovete? (What is your name? - formal) |
| oni/one/ona | zovu se | they are called | Zovu se... (Their names are...) |

**Examples:**
- Zovem se Jacksenn. (My name is Jacksenn.)
- Kako se zoveš? (What's your name? - informal)
- Kako se zovete? (What's your name? - formal)

### 3.3 Gender of Nouns

In Serbian, every noun has a **gender**: Masculine, Feminine, or Neuter. This is not about biological gender — it's a grammatical category that affects how **we** use adjectives, verbs, and pronouns.

#### How to Recognize Gender

| **Gender** | **Typical Ending** | **Examples** |
|-----------|-------------------|-------------|
| **Masculine** | consonant | pasoš (passport), hotel (hotel), grad (city) |
| **Feminine** | -a | granica (border), voda (water), mapa (map) |
| **Neuter** | -o, -e | selo (village), more (sea) |

**Why does gender matter?**

Gender affects:
1. **Adjectives:** lep hotel (nice hotel - masculine), lepa* plaža (nice beach - feminine), lepo* selo (nice village - neuter)
2. **Past tense verbs:** Bio sam (I was - masculine), Bila sam (I was - feminine)
3. **Pronouns:** on (he/it - masculine), ona (she/it - feminine), ono (it - neuter)

**\* Montenegrin (Ijekavian) variant:** "lep" → "lijep", "lepa" → "lijepa", "lepo" → "lijepo"

**Tip:** Don't worry about memorizing all genders right away. **We** will learn them naturally through practice. For now, just notice the pattern: **-a = usually feminine, consonant = usually masculine, -o/-e = usually neuter.**

### 3.4 Basic Question Words

| **Serbian (Ekavian)** | **Montenegrin (Ijekavian)** | **English** |
|----------------------|----------------------------|-------------|
| gde* | gdje* | where |

| kako | kako | how |

**\* Montenegrin (Ijekavian) variant:** "gde" → "gdje"

**Examples:**

## 5. Dialogues (Dialoge)

**We** learn best through real conversations. Here are four authentic dialogues that represent different arrival scenarios in Serbia or Montenegro.

### Dialogue 1: At the Border (Na granici)

**Border Officer (Granični policajac):** Dobar dan. Pasoš, molim.  
**Tourist (Turista):** Dobar dan. Evo pasoša.  
**Border Officer:** Hvala. Ko ste vi?  
**Tourist:** Ja sam turista.  
**Border Officer:** Gde* idete? / Gdje* idete?  
**Tourist:** Idem u Crnu Goru.  
**Border Officer:** Dobro. Hvala. Doviđenja*.  
**Tourist:** Hvala. Doviđenja*.  

**\* Montenegrin (Ijekavian) variant:** "gde" → "gdje", "doviđenja" → "doviđenja" (same)

**Translation:**
- **Border Officer:** Good day. Passport, please.
- **Tourist:** Good day. Here is the passport.
- **Border Officer:** Thank you. Who are you?
- **Tourist:** I am a tourist.
- **Border Officer:** Where are you going?
- **Tourist:** I am going to Montenegro.
- **Border Officer:** Good. Thank you. Goodbye.
- **Tourist:** Thank you. Goodbye.

### Dialogue 2: At the Rest Stop (Na benzinskoj pumpi)

**Tourist (Turista):** Zdravo. Gde* je toalet? / Zdravo. Gdje* je toalet?  
**Employee (Zaposleni):** Toalet je ovde*, levo*. / Toalet je ovdje*, lijevo*.  
**Tourist:** Hvala. Gde* je kafa? / Hvala. Gdje* je kafa?  
**Employee:** Kafa je u restoranu.  
**Tourist:** Hvala lepo*. / Hvala lijepo*.  
**Employee:** Molim.  

**\* Montenegrin (Ijekavian) variant:** "gde" → "gdje", "ovde" → "ovdje", "levo" → "lijevo", "lepo" → "lijepo"

**Translation:**
- **Tourist:** Hello. Where is the toilet?
- **Employee:** The toilet is here, on the left.
- **Tourist:** Thank you. Where is the coffee?
- **Employee:** Coffee is in the restaurant.
- **Tourist:** Thank you very much.
- **Employee:** You're welcome.

### Dialogue 3: At the Hotel (U hotelu)

**Receptionist (Recepcioner):** Dobar dan. Kako se zovete?  
**Tourist (Turista):** Dobar dan. Zovem se Ana.  
**Receptionist:** Drago mi je, Ana. Evo ključa. Soba je ovde*.  
**Tourist:** Hvala. Gde* je restoran? / Hvala. Gdje* je restoran?  
**Receptionist:** Restoran je levo*. / Restoran je lijevo*.  
**Tourist:** Hvala lepo*. / Hvala lijepo*.  
**Receptionist:** Molim.  

**\* Montenegrin (Ijekavian) variant:** "ovde" → "ovdje", "gde" → "gdje", "levo" → "lijevo", "lepo" → "lijepo"

**Translation:**
- **Receptionist:** Good day. What is your name?
- **Tourist:** Good day. My name is Ana.
- **Receptionist:** Nice to meet you, Ana. Here is the key. The room is here.
- **Tourist:** Thank you. Where is the restaurant?
- **Receptionist:** The restaurant is on the left.
- **Tourist:** Thank you very much.
- **Receptionist:** You're welcome.

### Dialogue 4: At the Tourist Information (U turističkoj informaciji)

**Tourist (Turista):** Dobar dan. Trebam mapu.  
**Employee (Zaposleni):** Dobar dan. Evo mape. Gde* idete? / Dobar dan. Evo mape. Gdje* idete?  
**Tourist:** Idem na plažu.  
**Employee:** Plaža je ovde*. / Plaža je ovdje*.  
**Tourist:** Hvala na informaciji.  
**Employee:** Molim. Doviđenja*.  
**Tourist:** Doviđenja*.  

**\* Montenegrin (Ijekavian) variant:** "gde" → "gdje", "ovde" → "ovdje", "doviđenja" → "doviđenja" (same)

**Translation:**
- **Tourist:** Good day. I need a map.
- **Employee:** Good day. Here is the map. Where are you going?
- **Tourist:** I am going to the beach.
- **Employee:** The beach is here.
- **Tourist:** Thank you for the information.
- **Employee:** You're welcome. Goodbye.
- **Tourist:** Goodbye.
