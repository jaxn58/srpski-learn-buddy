## 3. Grammar (Gramatika)

### A. Prepositions of Place - Describing Where We Are

Prepositions are small words that tell **us** where things are. In Serbian, they often affect the case of the noun, but for now, **we** will focus on the most common ones with the simplest case (Locative or Accusative, which we will treat as simple forms).

| Preposition | Meaning | Example (Serbian) | Example (English) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **u** | in | **u** stanu | in the apartment |
| **na** | on / at | **na** stolu | on the table |
| **pored** | next to | **pored** kuće | next to the house |
| **ispod** | under | **ispod** kreveta | under the bed |
| **iznad** | above | **iznad** vrata | above the door |

#### Practical Examples

*   **Ključ** je **na** stolu. (The **key** is **on** the table.)
*   **Mi** smo **u** kući. (**We** are **in** the house.)
*   **Banka** je **pored** stana. (The **bank** is **next to** the apartment.)

---

### B. Descriptive Adjectives - Making Our World Colorful

Adjectives are words that describe nouns. In Serbian, adjectives must agree with the noun they describe in **gender** (Masculine, Feminine, Neuter).

#### The Simple Adjective Rule

Most adjectives end in a consonant in the masculine form, **-a** in the feminine form, and **-o** in the neuter form.

| Adjective | Masculine (m.) | Feminine (f.) | Neuter (n.) | English |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **big** | velik | velika | veliko | big |
| **nice/beautiful** | lep / **lijep** | lepa / **ljepa** | lepo / **lijepo** | nice/beautiful |
| **old** | star | stara | staro | old |
| **new** | nov | nova | novo | new |

**Montenegrin Note:** In Montenegro, "lep/lepa/lepo" becomes "lijep/ljepa/lijepo" (Ijekavian variant).

#### Practical Examples with Gender Agreement

| Noun (Gender) | Adjective | Full Phrase (Serbian) | English |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Stan (m.) | nov | **nov** stan | a **new** apartment |
| Kuća (f.) | velika | **velika** kuća | a **big** house |
| Kupatilo (n.) | lepo | **lepo** kupatilo | a **nice** bathroom |
| Ključ (m.) | star | **star** ključ | an **old** key |

### B. Dialogue: Talking to a Landlord

**Scene:** Alex is viewing an apartment and talking to the landlord (Vlasnik).

| Role | Serbian | English |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Alex** | Dobar dan. Da li je ovo slobodan stan? | Good day. Is this apartment free? |
| **Landlord** | Dobar dan. Jeste. Izvolite. | Good day. Yes, it is. Come in. |
| **Alex** | Hvala. Stan je **lep**. Koliko košta kirija? | Thank you. The apartment is **nice**. How much does the rent cost? |
| **Landlord** | Kirija je **trista pedeset** evra. | The rent is **three hundred fifty** euros. |
| **Alex** | U redu. Gde je **kupatilo**? / **Gdje je kupatilo?** | Alright. Where is the **bathroom**? | **Montenegro:** "Gdje" |
| **Landlord** | Kupatilo je **pored** kuhinje. | The bathroom is **next to** the kitchen. |
| **Alex** | Sviđa mi se. Treba mi ugovor. | I like it. I need a contract. |
| **Landlord** | U redu. Doviđenja. | Alright. Goodbye. |

### C. Cultural Note: Housing Terminology and Customs

*   **Stan vs. Kuća:** *Stan* is the standard word for an apartment or flat, while *Kuća* is a house. Most expats start by renting a *stan*.
*   **Nameštaj:** The Ijekavian variant is **Namještaj**. This is a common word you will encounter when discussing furnished apartments.
*   **The Landlord:** The term *Vlasnik* (owner) is commonly used. Being polite and using formal address (*Vi*) is essential when negotiating a contract (*Ugovor*).
