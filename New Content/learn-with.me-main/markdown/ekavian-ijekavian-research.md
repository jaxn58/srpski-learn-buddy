## Key Facts

### Geographic Distribution

**Ekavian (Serbia):**
- Dominant in Serbia
- Uses "e" pronunciation

**Ijekavian (Montenegro, Bosnia, Croatia):**
- Mandatory in Montenegro
- Uses "je" or "ije" pronunciation

**Critical Rule:** Montenegro ONLY uses Ijekavian. Serbia uses both, but Ekavian is dominant.

---

## The Yat Reflex Rule

The historical Proto-Slavic vowel "ě" (called "yat" or "jat") evolved differently:

**Ekavian:** ě → e  
**Ijekavian:** ě → je or ije

## Words That DO NOT Change

**Important:** Not every "e" changes to "je/ije". Only the historical yat vowel changes.

**Examples of NO CHANGE:**
- **veče** → **veče** (evening) - NOT from yat
- **novac** → **novac** (money) - NO "e"
- **evro** → **evro** (euro) - NO "e"
- **pivo** → **pivo** (beer) - NO "e"
- **vino** → **vino** (wine) - NO "e"
