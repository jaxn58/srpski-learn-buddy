# Unit 16: Practice Examples & Dialogues

## 📝 Dialogue 1: Looking for an Apartment

**Scenario:** Steve is meeting with a landlord (gazda) to view an apartment in Belgrade.

---

**Gazda:** Dobar dan! Vi ste Stiv, zar ne?  
*Good day! You are Steve, right?*

**Stiv:** Da, ja sam. Drago mi je.  
*Yes, I am. Nice to meet you.*

**Gazda:** Drago mi je. Hoćete da vidite stan?  
*Nice to meet you. Would you like to see the apartment?*

**Stiv:** Da, molim vas. Koliko soba ima stan?  
*Yes, please. How many rooms does the apartment have?*

**Gazda:** Stan ima dve sobe, kuhinju i kupatilo.  
*The apartment has two rooms, a kitchen, and a bathroom.*

**Stiv:** Odlično! Kolika je kirija?  
*Excellent! How much is the rent?*

**Gazda:** Kirija je trideset hiljada dinara mesečno.  
*The rent is thirty thousand dinars per month.*

**Stiv:** Mogu li da priđem prozoru?  
*May I come to the window?*

**Gazda:** Naravno! Prozoru možete da priđete.  
*Of course! You can come to the window.*

## 📝 Dialogue 2: Describing the New Apartment to a Friend

**Scenario:** Steve is telling his friend Marko about his new apartment over coffee.

---

**Marko:** Kako ti se dopada novi stan?  
*How do you like the new apartment?*

**Stiv:** Dopada mi se jako! Blizu je centru.  
*I like it a lot! It's close to the center.*

**Marko:** Šta si kupio za stan?  
*What did you buy for the apartment?*

**Stiv:** Kupio sam krevet, sto, stolice i lampu.  
*I bought a bed, table, chairs, and a lamp.*

**Marko:** Treba li ti pomoć sa nameštajem?  
*Do you need help with the furniture?*

**Stiv:** Da, hvala! Sutra ću dati ključeve mojim prijateljima.  
*Yes, thank you! Tomorrow I will give the keys to my friends.*

**Marko:** Super! Mogu da pomognem tebi i tvojim prijateljima.  
*Great! I can help you and your friends.*

**Stiv:** Hvala Marku! Ti si dobar prijatelj.  
*Thank you, Marko! You are a good friend.*

## 📝 Dialogue 3: Moving Day

**Scenario:** Steve and his friends are moving furniture into the new apartment.

---

**Stiv:** Gde da stavimo krevet?  
*Where should we put the bed?*

**Ana:** Krevet možemo da stavimo u spavaćoj sobi.  
*We can put the bed in the bedroom.*

**Stiv:** A gde da stavimo sto?  
*And where should we put the table?*

**Marko:** Sto ide u dnevnoj sobi, prema prozoru.  
*The table goes in the living room, towards the window.*

**Ana:** Stive, dao si ključ Mileni?  
*Steve, did you give the key to Milena?*

**Stiv:** Da, dao sam joj ključ. Ona dolazi kasnije.  
*Yes, I gave her the key. She's coming later.*

**Marko:** Trebamo još stolice. Mogu da ih donesem svojoj komšiji.  
*We need more chairs. I can bring them to my neighbor.*

**Stiv:** Ne, ne! Možeš da doneseš stolice mom stanu!  
*No, no! You can bring the chairs to my apartment!*

*(Svi se smeju)*  
*(Everyone laughs)*

## 💡 Grammar Tip: When to use Dative

### 1. **Indirect Object** (To whom? For whom?)
- **Dajem ključ prijatelju.** (I'm giving the key to a friend.)
- **Kupujem poklon sestri.** (I'm buying a gift for my sister.)

### 2. **With certain verbs** (pomoći, približiti se, pristupiti)
- **Pomažem majci.** (I'm helping my mother.)
- **Prilazim kući.** (I'm approaching the house.)

### 3. **With prepositions** (ka, prema, k)
- **Idem ka školi.** (I'm going to school.)
- **Hodam prema centru.** (I'm walking towards the center.)

### 4. **Direction/Goal** (especially with motion verbs)
- **Trčim kući.** (I'm running home.)
- **Idem prijatelju.** (I'm going to my friend's place.)

## 📖 Cultural Note: Living in Serbia

**Renting Apartments:**
In Serbia, rent (kirija) is typically paid monthly. Many Belgraders prefer to live in apartments (stan) rather than houses (kuća), especially in city centers. It's common to have a "gazda" (landlord) rather than dealing with a large rental company.

**Helping Friends Move:**
In Serbian culture, it's very common for friends to help each other move. The person moving typically provides food and drinks, and sometimes a small celebration after the move is complete!

**Key Serbian Phrases for Moving:**
- **Sve najbolje u novom domu!** - All the best in your new home!
- **Srećno u novom stanu!** - Good luck in your new apartment!
- **Lep stan!** - Nice apartment!
