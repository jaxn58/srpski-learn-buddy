# Can-Do Inventory: Serbian A1.1 to B1

**Status**: Draft for approval (2026-09-16), Phase 1a of the Content Studio Upgrade plan.  
**Purpose**: Single source of learning objectives for unit briefs. Every unit brief lists 4 to 6 statements from this inventory under `Can-Do Statements:`; the Creator turns them into the unit's Learning Objectives and the exercises must test them.  
**Basis**: CEFR Companion Volume descriptors (spoken interaction, spoken production, listening, reading, written interaction), adapted to the situational strands of this course and to life in Serbia, with Montenegro as the recurring holiday and coast context.  
**Conventions**: IDs are stable (`A1.1-ARR-01`). Strand codes: ARR (Arrival & Travel), SET (Settling In), DAY (Everyday Life), SOC (People & Social), BIZ (Work & Business), DIG (Digital & Services), GEN (cross-strand language skill). `Target unit` refers to `UNIT_MAP.md`; "(R)" marks a later unit that recycles the statement without new grammar. Review and checkpoint units never introduce a statement; they only recycle.

## Using this inventory in briefs

1. Pick 4 to 6 statements for the unit, at least one GEN and at least one situational strand.
2. Copy the statement text verbatim into the brief; the Creator rewrites it into the "we will be able to" form.
3. Every statement must be testable by at least one exercise item; the brief's `Exercise focus:` names the category.
4. Recycled statements marked (R) appear in a later unit with a new situation but no new grammar.
5. Review and checkpoint units list only recycled statements.
