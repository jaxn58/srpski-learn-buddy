# Unit Map: Serbian A1.1 to B1 (75 units)

**Status**: Draft for approval (2026-09-16), Phase 1a of the Content Studio Upgrade plan.  
**Purpose**: List of all units: module placement, CEFR level, unit type, primary grammar target, recycled grammar, and an example situation. Unit briefings are written from this map together with `CAN_DO_INVENTORY.md`. Earlier planning sketches are superseded; the only existing content is Units 1 to 4 in the Dev database, which keep their numbers.  
**What is binding and what is not** (decision 2026-09-16): only the **language scaffold** is binding: CEFR level, unit type, primary grammar, recycle, and the Can-Do statements, because the units build on each other linguistically. **Strand, working title, situation, setting and chunks are examples**: they show one way to reach the language goal and may be replaced freely by the author's briefing (topic, places, characters, scenes). The Briefing Assistant uses the example only when the author gives no description, and says so.  
**Rules** (from `CURRICULUM_FRAMEWORK.md`): unit numbers are append-only and equal the learning order; U001–U004 keep their numbers and core topics; one primary grammar target per standard unit; 20–30 new words; review units add no words or grammar; every unit carries `moduleMetadataId` of its module. Default setting is Serbia (Belgrade, Novi Sad, Niš, Subotica, Kragujevac; prices in dinars). Montenegro appears as the recurring holiday and coast context of the ARR strand (Tivat airport, Budva, Kotor; prices in euros) and through automatic Montenegrin variant notes in the vocabulary. Administrative units are set in Serbia (MUP, opština, eUprava, APR); their briefs note where Montenegro differs. Teaching norm is Ekavian throughout.

Column legend: **Type** standard | review | checkpoint | exam (binding). **Strand** ARR Arrival & Travel, SET Settling In, DAY Everyday Life, SOC People & Social, BIZ Work & Business, DIG Digital & Services, GEN cross-strand (example). **Working title** and **Situation** are examples. **Primary grammar** and **Recycle** are binding. **Chunks** lists recommended fixed phrases the unit may use before the grammar is taught (Chunk-Policy); other chunks that fit the author's scenes are allowed as long as they stay within the level.
